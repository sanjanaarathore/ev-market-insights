Problem Statement

Understanding the Multi-Polar EV Industrial Landscape

The global electric vehicle transition is accelerating across consumer and commercial sectors. However, automotive stakeholders face fragmented intelligence regarding cross-manufacturer competitiveness, real-world battery longevity, manufacturing trade flows, and the economic determinants of price.

This project addresses this critical gap through a rigorous, full-lifecycle empirical analysis—spanning dataset quality auditing, domain-grounded preprocessing, interactive Tableau dashboarding, and cloud-ready web deployment.

Project Objectives

Core Analytical Mandates

  • Manufacturer Benchmarking: Evaluate portfolio diversity and sales across 200 global OEMs.
  • Category Stratification: Analyze performance across 15 vehicle body categories from scooters to transit buses.
  • Battery Chemistry Longevity: Compare cycle life and degradation across NMC, LFP, NCA, Sodium-Ion, and Solid-State cells.
  • Economic Feasibility: Model retail MSRP cost determinants against battery capacity and range.
  • Climate Impact Assessment: Quantify net carbon abatement (tons CO2 saved per vehicle per year vs ICE).

Research Findings

Core Analytical Discoveries & Business Takeaways

Empirically grounded findings derived from comprehensive statistical modeling of the 300,000-vehicle fleet.

01

Commercial Transit is the Top Decarbonization Lever

Electric Buses (9.04 tons CO2/yr) and Freight Trucks (8.99 tons CO2/yr) displace nearly 3x the carbon emissions of standard passenger vehicles (3.04 tons/yr). Electrifying municipal and freight transit yields the highest return on climate capital.

9.04 t/yr CO2 Abatement
02

Battery Capacity Dictates Capital Cost

Traction battery gross capacity exhibits a decisive r = +0.8373 correlation with retail price ($ USD). Installed kilowatt-hours represent the primary cost component in vehicle manufacturing, underscoring battery pack economics as the core battleground for OEM margins.

r = +0.8373 Price Correlation
03

The NMC vs LFP Cathode Chemistry Duopoly

NMC (33.84%) and LFP (32.05%) collectively command 65.89% of global vehicles. LFP delivers a 39% superior cycle durability (2,501 cycles) at lower 5-year degradation (10.1%), while Solid-State delivers 2,998 cycles in premium models.

65.89% Duopoly Market Share
04

Asia Commands 50.1% of Vehicle Assembly

Global manufacturing is multi-polar, but Asian industrial clusters (India 10.1%, South Korea 10.1%, China 10.1%, Vietnam 10.0%, Japan 9.9%) collectively assemble 50.13% of all EV models, outpacing Europe (29.9%) and North America (10.0%).

50.13% Asian Assembly Share
05

Universal Customer Satisfaction Clustering

Across all 15 vehicle categories, average customer satisfaction remains tightly clustered between 4.085★ and 4.094★ out of 5.0. Budget two-wheelers satisfy commuter utility expectations just as effectively as six-figure luxury sedans satisfy executive luxury.

4.09 / 5.0 ★ Fleet Average
06

Robust Multi-Year Sales Continuity

Fleet sales across model vintages (2018 through 2026) exhibit consistent annual volumes between 255M and 265M units per year. Peak volume was registered in 2019 (265.7M) and matched in 2024 (264.9M), confirming structural adoption permanence.

265.7M Units Vintage Peak
